# TRACE-v1 source and coverage flags

## Authority and scope

- Scope is Cambridge 9618 Paper 1 for the 2026 examination year.
- The 2026 syllabus is the authority for all 99 objectives and 205 REQUIRED atomic requirements.
- Accepted QP/MS evidence supplies historical official assessment coverage at atomic-unit level.
- Learning-unit grouping, topological order, HARD prerequisite edges and original assessment briefs are AlgoCore pedagogy.

## Assessment split

- `AUTHOR_POOL`: 701 accepted official assessment units. These may be referenced by later authoring workflows under their source constraints.
- `CONTROLLED_CHECK`: 192 accepted official assessment units. These remain historical coverage/check evidence and must never enter an author-facing allowlist or example pool.
- Fifteen requirements have no author-available official unit: 12 have no official evidence and 3 have controlled-check-only evidence. Exactly 15 `ALGCORE_ORIGINAL` briefs record the later assessment-design need without writing a full question, answer or solution.

## Coursebook

- Accepted foundation rows report 83 objectives as `NO_VERIFIED_BOOK_SUPPORT`, 14 as `VERIFIED_EXCERPT`, one as `VERIFIED_WITH_AUTHORITY_CONFLICT`, and one as `VERIFIED_WITH_CAUTION`.
- `NO_VERIFIED_BOOK_SUPPORT` remains explicit for 165 atomic requirements. TOC inference and derived files are not promoted to source authority.
- Where book wording conflicts or needs caution, the syllabus remains controlling authority.

## Patterns, equivalence and glossary

- The final catalog contains 504 patterns across 893 occurrences and 824 accepted equivalence components. Three occurrences depend on a `NEEDS_REVIEW` pattern status; those requirement rows stay flagged.
- The glossary registry contains 96 accepted English/VI candidate records and the command-word register contains 27 records. Their `en_status`, `vi_status`, reviewer status and reconciliation status remain explicit. This packet does not claim complete bilingual lesson parity.

## Unresolved context

- All 128 Stage 1 unresolved context-only records are preserved in `LEARNING_MAP.json`. They remain ineligible as assessment units, for item scoring and for marking claims.

## Stage boundary

- This packet proves planned coverage and reference closure only. It contains no lesson prose, full question, answer, solution, translation, app change or Stage 3 artifact.
