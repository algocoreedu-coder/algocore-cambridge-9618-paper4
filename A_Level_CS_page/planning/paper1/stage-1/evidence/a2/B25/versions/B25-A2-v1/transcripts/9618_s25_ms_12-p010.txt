IPv4 is made of four groups of 8-bit numbers separated by full stops. Each 
IPv4 address is 32 bits.  
 
IPv6 is made of eight groups of 4 hexadecimal numbers separated by colons. 
 
Multiple consecutive groups of zeros can be replaced with a double colon. 
Each IPv6 address is 128 bits. 
 
A dynamic IP address can change each time the computer connects to a 
network. 
 
A private IP address can only be accessed by other devices in the same LAN 
and is assigned by the router within the LAN.
