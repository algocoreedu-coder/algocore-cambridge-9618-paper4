Selecting the customer ID, customer name and sum of TotalCost with 
appropriate identifier 
• 
FROM clause with suitable join of tables (ON or WHERE) 
• 
ORDER.Paid = FALSE condition (with correct key word) 
• 
GROUP BY condition 
 
e.g. 
SELECT CUSTOMER.CustomerID, CUSTOMER.Name,  
Sum(ORDER.TotalCost) AS TotalOwed 
FROM CUSTOMER INNER JOIN ORDER  
ON CUSTOMER.CustomerID = ORDER.CustomerID 
WHERE ORDER.Paid = FALSE  
GROUP BY CUSTOMER.CustomerID; 
 
Alternative JOIN Statement 
 
SELECT CUSTOMER.CustomerID, CUSTOMER.Name,  
Sum(ORDER.TotalCost) AS TotalOwed 
FROM CUSTOMER,ORDER  
WHERE CUSTOMER.CustomerID = ORDER.CustomerID 
AND ORDER.Paid = FALSE 
GROUP BY CUSTOMER.CustomerID;
