At the start / end of FE cycle the interrupt register is checked 
• 
The priority of any interrupts waiting is checked  
• 
if the priority of the interrupt is higher than the current process 
• 
the contents of the registers are stored on the stack 
• 
The relevant Interrupt Service Routine (ISR) / interrupt handler is called to 
process the interrupt 
• 
When the ISR has finished, a further check is made for higher priority 
interrupts 
• 
if no more interrupts of higher priority, the register contents are restored 
and the next FE cycle continues
