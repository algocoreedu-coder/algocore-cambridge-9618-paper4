# Lead source adjudications

Status: **LEAD_REVIEWED**. These decisions close the two arithmetic ambiguities raised by S4-S1 without inventing an allocation that the printed source does not establish.

## S4-S1-DEC-001 — Winter 2021, question 2(e)

Direct inspection of pages 12 of `9618_w21_ms_41` and `9618_w21_ms_42` confirms two exception bullets followed by nine implementation bullets with a printed `Max 7`, while the marks column is 8. The canonical map therefore keeps all eleven criteria, leaves criterion-level values unassigned, and applies the official part-level ceiling of 8. No Stage 4 output may sum the displayed bullets to 9 or claim which criterion loses a mark.

Affected parts: `9618_w21_41_2(e)`, `9618_w21_42_2(e)`.

## S4-S1-DEC-002 — Winter 2022, question 1(b)

Direct inspection of pages 3 of `9618_w22_ms_41` and `9618_w22_ms_43` confirms five file-processing bullets followed by two exception bullets, while the marks column is 6. The canonical map keeps all seven criteria, leaves criterion-level values unassigned, and applies the official part-level ceiling of 6. It does not assume that the two exception bullets combine into one mark because the facsimile does not state that rule.

Affected parts: `9618_w22_41_1(b)`, `9618_w22_43_1(b)`.

These are conservative editorial dispositions. They preserve every criterion and the official total while avoiding an unsupported mark split.
