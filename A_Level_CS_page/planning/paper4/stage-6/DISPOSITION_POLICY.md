# Stage 6 disposition policy

Disposition is an explicit reviewed outcome, never a way to reduce a denominator. It is allowed for Stage 3 support-only requirements, non-code support concepts, or a source item whose evidence is documentary/facsimile under the locked upstream authority. The record must list every affected ID, exact reason, authority label, owner, Lead reviewer, evidence hash and independent recheck command. Required Stage 5 executable obligations cannot be waived by a prose disposition.
